# 🧠 AFTERMIND

### AI-Powered Organizational Memory & Decision Intelligence Platform

**"Don't Just Store Information. Remember, Understand and Act on It."**

## 📌 About the Project

AFTERMIND is an AI-powered business memory platform that collects, processes, understands, and connects information from multiple sources.

It transforms scattered business information such as documents, meeting recordings, voice notes, and conversations into structured, searchable organizational knowledge.

The platform helps organizations preserve important discussions, retrieve historical decisions, and track business commitments using Artificial Intelligence.

## ❗ Problem Statement

Organizations generate large amounts of information across different platforms, making it difficult to:

* Retrieve previous discussions and decisions.
* Preserve important organizational knowledge.
* Track responsibilities and deadlines.
* Find relevant information across multiple sources.
* Avoid repeated discussions and missed commitments.

## 💡 Key Features

* **Multi-Source Data Ingestion:** Extract information from PDF, DOCX, TXT, MP3, WAV, and M4A files.
* **Intelligent Memory Extraction:** Identify important information, entities, decisions, and business context.
* **Connected Long-Term Memory:** Preserve and connect historical business knowledge.
* **Semantic Search:** Retrieve information based on meaning using natural-language queries.
* **Smart Commitment Tracker:** Identify tasks, responsibilities, decisions, and deadlines.
* **AI Business Assistant:** Interact with organizational memory through natural-language conversations.
* **Centralized Dashboard:** Access business information, memories, and commitments.
